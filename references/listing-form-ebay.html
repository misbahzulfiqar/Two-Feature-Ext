Comparing the campaign strategies

General

A cost-per-sale solution that gives your listings basic access to ad placements across eBay, boosting the visibility of your listings over non-promoted items.

Basic access to ad placements

Basic access means listings will be included in ad placements across eBay, helping you reach more buyers than you can with non-promoted listings.

Great for accelerating sales

Helps accelerate sales on listings with moderate competition. Consider using the general strategy to promote a wide range of inventory as you pay only when an item sells.

Pay per sale

You’ll pay an ad rate only when your item sells. The ad rate is a percentage of the total sale amount (including price, shipping, taxes, and fees).

Priority

A cost-per-click solution that gives your listings preferential ranking in ad placements across eBay, boosting the visibility of your listings over listings promoted with a general strategy.

Unlock the top slot of search and preferential ranking

With exclusive access to the top search slot and preferential ranking in all placements, you can secure more visible spots on eBay and reach more buyers than with the general strategy.

Great for taking sales to the next level

Helps multiply sales on listings in highly competitive categories. Consider promoting high-demand and multi-quantity items with the priority strategy to help drive the most sales.

Pay per click

You’ll only pay for clicks on your ads based on your campaign’s target daily budget. This can vary depending on the day, so you could spend up to double this amount, or less so it averages out over a calendar month. Learn more

Top tip

These campaign strategies can be used together or on their own to help you meet your selling goals. After you promote your listing, you can monitor and manage performance via the Advertising Dashboard - opens in new window or tab.
